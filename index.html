How it works

  1. Save Kindred's WhatsApp number, +91 85718 94580, and send "Hi".
  2. Forward any report: a PDF or a photo.
  3. Kindred keeps it under the right person and date. Your family can see it too.

What you can do

  • Keep every lab report, prescription and discharge summary for yourself and the people you care for.
  • See each test result over time, with the normal range next to it.
  • Log blood pressure and sugar, with reminders for everyone who looks after that person.
  • Share a person's records with family, and remove access at any time.
  • If you say yes, AI explains a report in plain words and helps you prepare for the doctor visit. You choose; you can change it any time.
  • Use Kindred in English or 12 Indian languages.
