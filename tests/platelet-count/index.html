Platelet count test: normal range, high and low meaning

Platelets are small cells in the blood that help it clot and stop bleeding.

Usual range

  • Adults: about 1.5 to 4.5 lakh per µL (150,000 to 450,000)

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is low

A low count is common with dengue and other viral fevers. It can also come from some medicines, liver disease or bone-marrow problems. A very low count can cause bleeding.

If it is high

A high count can follow an infection, inflammation, iron deficiency or surgery. Rarely, it comes from a bone-marrow condition.

When to see a doctor soon

See a doctor the same day if platelets are below about 50,000, or if there is bleeding from the gums or nose, blood in urine or stool, or new red spots on the skin.

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Why is my platelet count low or high?
  • When should I repeat the test?
  • Should I stop any medicine, such as painkillers or blood thinners?
