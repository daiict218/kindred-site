Vitamin D (25-OH) test: normal range, high and low meaning

Vitamin D helps the body take in calcium for strong bones and muscles. The blood test measures 25-hydroxy vitamin D.

Usual range

  • Deficient: below 20 ng/mL
  • Insufficient: 20 to 29 ng/mL
  • Enough: 30 to 100 ng/mL
  • Too high: above 100 ng/mL

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is low

Low vitamin D is very common in India, even with plenty of sunshine. It can cause bone and muscle pain and weakness and, over years, weak bones (osteoporosis).

If it is high

High vitamin D almost always comes from taking too many supplements. Very high levels can raise calcium and harm the kidneys.

When to see a doctor soon

Ask your doctor for the right dose before you take high-dose vitamin D (for example 60,000 IU sachets or capsules).

Questions to ask your doctor

  • What dose should I take, and for how long?
  • When should I recheck?
  • Should I check my calcium too?
