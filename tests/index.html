Lab test guides

What your blood test results mean, in plain words: the usual range, what high and low can mean, and what to ask your doctor.

Blood count (CBC)

  • Haemoglobin (Hb)
  • Total WBC count (TLC)
  • Platelet count

Sugar (diabetes)

  • Fasting blood sugar (FBS)
  • Post-meal blood sugar (PPBS)
  • HbA1c

Cholesterol (lipid profile)

  • Total cholesterol
  • LDL cholesterol
  • HDL cholesterol
  • Triglycerides

Kidney

  • Creatinine
  • Urea
  • Uric acid

Liver

  • SGPT (ALT)
  • SGOT (AST)
  • Bilirubin

Thyroid

  • TSH (thyroid test)

Vitamins and minerals

  • Vitamin D (25-OH)
  • Vitamin B12
  • Ferritin
  • Calcium

Inflammation and joints

  • ESR
  • CRP (C-reactive protein)
  • Rheumatoid factor (RF)
