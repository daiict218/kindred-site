Uric acid test: normal range, high and low meaning

Uric acid is a waste product from the breakdown of purines. Purines are made in the body and are also in foods such as red meat, organ meat, some seafood and alcohol, especially beer.

Usual range

  • Men: about 3.5 to 7.2 mg/dL
  • Women: about 2.6 to 6.0 mg/dL

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is high

High uric acid can cause gout (a sudden, very painful swelling of a joint, often the big toe) and kidney stones. Many people with high uric acid have no symptoms and do not need medicine.

If it is low

Low uric acid is rarely a concern.

When to see a doctor soon

See a doctor if you have a sudden painful, red, swollen joint, or high uric acid with kidney stones.

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Do I need medicine, or only changes in food and drink?
  • Which foods and drinks should I limit?
