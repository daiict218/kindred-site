SGOT (AST) test: normal range, high and low meaning

SGOT, also called AST, is an enzyme found in the liver, heart and muscles.

Usual range

  • Adults: usually up to about 40 U/L (labs differ)

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is high

High SGOT can come from liver problems (fatty liver, alcohol, hepatitis), and also from a muscle injury or heavy exercise. If SGOT is much higher than SGPT, alcohol is a common cause.

If it is low

A low SGOT is not a concern.

When to see a doctor soon

See a doctor soon if SGOT is more than 3 times the upper limit, or if there are yellow eyes or skin, or dark urine.

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Is my SGPT also high?
  • Could exercise or a muscle injury explain it?
  • Do I need more liver tests?
