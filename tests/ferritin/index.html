Ferritin test: normal range, high and low meaning

Ferritin shows how much iron your body has stored. It is the best single test for iron deficiency.

Usual range

  • Men: about 30 to 400 ng/mL
  • Women: about 15 to 150 ng/mL
  • Below about 30 ng/mL usually means low iron stores

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is low

Low ferritin means low iron stores, the most common cause of anaemia in India. Causes include too little iron in food, heavy periods, pregnancy and blood loss from the gut.

If it is high

High ferritin can come from inflammation, infection, fatty liver or alcohol, and rarely from iron overload. By itself, it does not mean there is too much iron.

When to see a doctor soon

See a doctor if ferritin is low with tiredness or low haemoglobin, or very high (above about 1,000 ng/mL).

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Do I need iron tablets or an iron injection?
  • What is causing my low iron?
  • When should I recheck?
