Lab test guides

Total cholesterol test: normal range, high and low meaning

Total cholesterol is all the cholesterol in your blood: the 'bad' LDL, the 'good' HDL and the rest.

Usual range

  • Desirable: below 200 mg/dL
  • Borderline high: 200 to 239 mg/dL
  • High: 240 mg/dL or more

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is high

High cholesterol has no symptoms, but over years it can block blood vessels and lead to a heart attack or stroke. Your LDL, HDL and other risks (BP, sugar, smoking, heart disease in the family) matter more than the total alone.

If it is low

Low cholesterol is rarely a problem by itself.

When to see a doctor soon

Talk to a doctor if total cholesterol is 240 mg/dL or more, or LDL is high, especially with diabetes, high BP or heart disease in the family.

Questions to ask your doctor

  • What is my overall heart risk?
  • Do I need medicine, or can I start with diet and exercise?
  • When should I repeat the lipid profile?
