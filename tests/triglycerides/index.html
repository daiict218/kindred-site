Triglycerides test: normal range, high and low meaning

Triglycerides are a type of fat in the blood. They go up with sugar, refined flour (maida), alcohol and extra weight.

Usual range

  • Normal: below 150 mg/dL
  • Borderline high: 150 to 199 mg/dL
  • High: 200 to 499 mg/dL
  • Very high: 500 mg/dL or more

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is high

High triglycerides raise heart risk and often come with diabetes, fatty liver and extra weight. Very high levels (500 or more) can cause pancreatitis, a painful swelling of the pancreas.

If it is low

Low triglycerides are rarely a problem.

When to see a doctor soon

See a doctor soon if triglycerides are 500 mg/dL or more, or if there is severe pain in the upper belly.

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Was this a fasting test? Should I repeat it fasting?
  • Should I check my sugar and liver?
  • Do I need medicine?
