Creatinine test: normal range, high and low meaning

Creatinine is a waste product from the muscles. The kidneys clean it out of the blood, so its level shows how well the kidneys work.

Usual range

  • Men: about 0.7 to 1.3 mg/dL
  • Women: about 0.6 to 1.1 mg/dL
  • It depends on muscle mass: it is lower in older and thin people

Ranges differ between labs, and by age and sex. Use the range printed on your report.

If it is high

High creatinine can mean the kidneys are not cleaning the blood well. Causes include too little water, some painkillers, diabetes, high BP and kidney disease. Heavy exercise or a lot of meat before the test can raise it a little.

If it is low

Low creatinine usually just means low muscle mass.

When to see a doctor soon

See a doctor soon if creatinine is above about 2 mg/dL or rising quickly, or if there is swelling, very little urine or breathlessness.

Questions to ask your doctor

  • What is my eGFR (kidney function score)?
  • Should I stop any painkillers or other medicines?
  • Do I need a urine test for protein?

Also printed on reports as

  • Serum Creatinine
  • S. Creatinine
  • Creatinine, Serum
